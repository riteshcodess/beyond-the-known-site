A private, owner-operated software tool used to prepare, publish and review videos for the BEYOND THE KNOWN YouTube channel.

What this application does

Beyond The Known Automation helps the channel owner manage the channel's video workflow. With the owner's permission, it connects to the owner's YouTube account to upload videos, check publishing status, and read channel and video analytics.

The application is for the channel owner's own use. It is not a public video-upload service, is not offered as a product to other YouTube creators, and does not let unrelated users access the channel.

Google and YouTube access

The application requests only the YouTube permissions needed for video uploads, read-only channel information, and read-only YouTube Analytics. Access is granted through Google's OAuth consent flow and can be revoked by the account owner at any time.
