Beyond The Known Automation

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Beyond The Known Automation handles information when its owner connects a Google/YouTube account. The application is a private tool operated by the owner of the BEYOND THE KNOWN YouTube channel; it is not a public service for third-party users.

1. Information accessed

When the account owner authorizes the application, it may access the following information through Google and YouTube APIs, limited by the permissions shown on Google's consent screen:

  • YouTube video upload permission: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube read-only permission: basic channel information and read-only access needed to identify the authorized channel and inspect video or channel status.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only permission: performance metrics for the owner's channel and videos, such as views, engaged views, average view duration, average view percentage, likes, comments, shares, and subscriber gains or losses, where available through the API.

The application does not request permission to edit, delete, or manage unrelated Google account content.

2. How information is used

Information accessed through the APIs is used only to operate the owner's YouTube workflow: identify the connected channel, upload videos selected by the owner, confirm upload or processing status, and review channel/video performance. Analytics are used to understand the performance of the owner's own published content and improve future channel decisions.

3. Storage and protection

OAuth credentials and workflow records are stored in the owner's private local automation environment and its configured application storage. Credentials are used to maintain the authorized connection and are not intended to be exposed publicly. The owner is responsible for securing the computer, local files, backups, and any configured infrastructure used by the workflow.

The application does not operate a public account system or a shared database for unrelated users. No information accessed through Google APIs is sold. Information is not shared with advertisers. Data may be transmitted to Google/YouTube as necessary to make authorized API requests and to infrastructure providers the owner has configured to run or store the application. Those providers process data as needed to provide their services.

4. Data retention and deletion

OAuth credentials remain stored in the owner's environment until the owner deletes them or revokes access. Workflow and analytics records may be retained locally to support channel operations and performance comparisons. The owner can delete locally stored records and revoke the application's Google access. Revoking access prevents future API access unless the account owner authorizes the application again.

To revoke access, visit Google Account third-party connections, select the application, and remove its access. To request information about this policy or the application's handling of data, contact the email below.

5. Data sharing

The application does not sell personal information or use YouTube API data for advertising. It does not disclose such data to third parties except as needed to make the requested Google/YouTube API calls, operate infrastructure configured by the owner, comply with applicable law, or protect the security of the application and its users.

6. Children's privacy

This application is an owner-operated channel management tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a public registration process.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when updates are made.

8. Contact
